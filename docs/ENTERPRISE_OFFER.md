# OpsPilot Managed Operations Automation - Offer Blueprint

**Commercial model:** Hybrid SaaS + implementation + managed automation  
**Target recurring fee:** USD $2,000 per customer per month  
**Status:** Offer hypothesis for customer discovery and paid-pilot validation

## 1. Who this is for

An operations team with recurring, document-heavy or system-to-system processes that currently require substantial manual handling, copying, checking, reporting, or follow-up.

Suitable customers have:
- Meaningful recurring process volume.
- A clear process owner and economic buyer.
- Accessible source files and systems.
- A process that can be mapped into explicit rules and review steps.
- A measurable baseline for time, cost, errors, or turnaround.

This offer is not intended for businesses needing only occasional file conversion or unlimited bespoke software development.

## 2. The promise

OpsPilot will design, implement, and operate an agreed set of business workflows using a hosted automation platform, with visibility into execution, exceptions, and measured outcomes.

Sell an agreed business result, not a promise that every process can be fully automated.

## 3. Proposed monthly service envelope

The following must be finalized per customer after discovery and cost modelling. Do not represent these as already available product features.

- Customer workspace for agreed named users.
- An agreed number of production workflows.
- Defined monthly execution and document/page allowances.
- Agreed integration endpoints and supported file types.
- Workflow monitoring and failure notifications.
- Agreed exception triage and operator support.
- Monthly usage and outcome review.
- Defined support hours and response targets.
- A documented change-request process.

Exclude unlimited executions, unlimited storage, unrestricted custom integrations, unlimited workflow changes, and 24/7 support unless separately priced and staffed.

## 4. Fees and contracting

### Recurring subscription

**Target:** USD $2,000/month per customer, invoiced monthly or annually under an agreed contract.

This target price is not validated. Confirm willingness to pay with paid pilots and ensure expected customer value materially exceeds the fee.

### Implementation and onboarding

Quote separately after process discovery. Estimate:
- Process mapping and solution design.
- Workflow configuration and testing.
- Data mapping and migration.
- Integration setup and credential coordination.
- User training and acceptance testing.
- Production cutover and hypercare.

Do not commit to a fixed implementation fee before scope and delivery effort are known. A pilot may be separately paid and optionally credited toward implementation or the first contract.

### Contract controls

Every statement of work should define:
- Workflows and process boundaries included.
- Customer responsibilities, data access, and system permissions.
- Deliverables and acceptance criteria.
- Usage allowances and overage pricing.
- Support hours, response targets, and exclusions.
- Data retention, export, deletion, and subprocessors.
- Change requests and additional workflow pricing.
- Contract term, renewal, cancellation, and payment terms.
- Security responsibilities and incident communication.

Obtain legal review for customer contracts and data-processing terms before international production use.

## 5. Pilot structure

A pilot should validate one workflow, not attempt a full enterprise transformation.

**Step 1 - Baseline:** document monthly volume, current staff time, error/rework rates, turnaround, and exception types.

**Step 2 - Scope:** agree source files/systems, workflow steps, human approvals, output, data sensitivity, and exclusions.

**Step 3 - Build:** configure a reusable workflow using synthetic or customer-approved limited data, with step-level logging and exception handling.

**Step 4 - Test:** run representative normal, invalid, duplicate, incomplete, and failure cases.

**Step 5 - Measure:** compare actual results with baseline. Report successful throughput, exception rate, processing time, manual review time, and support effort.

**Step 6 - Decide:** convert to a recurring contract only if the customer confirms value, security requirements are met, and delivery economics are sustainable.

Estimated monthly hours released:

`(baseline minutes per item - pilot minutes per item) * monthly volume / 60`

Translate released hours into financial value only with the customer's agreement on how capacity is redeployed or costs are reduced. Include ongoing platform, AI/API, and support costs in gross-margin analysis.

## 7. Delivery responsibilities

**Customer provides:** process owner, authorized system access, sample/approved data, business rules, acceptance reviewers, and timely decisions.

**OpsPilot provides:** discovery facilitation, workflow design, agreed implementation, testing evidence, onboarding, monitoring within the contracted scope, exception communication, and periodic outcome reporting.

**Shared responsibility:** validate extracted data, approve business rules, manage changes to source systems, and review exceptions before downstream business decisions.

## 8. Qualification checklist

Proceed to proposal only when:
- A real recurring process has been observed.
- The buyer and budget owner are known.
- Monthly volume and manual effort are quantified.
- Required systems and data access are feasible.
- Sensitive data and security obligations are understood.
- A measurable pilot outcome is agreed.
- Expected recurring value supports the target fee.
- Implementation and ongoing support effort can be delivered profitably.

**Commercial rule:** Never guarantee a $2,000 outcome, accuracy percentage, or labor saving without evidence. Sell a scoped process, prove the result, then expand.
